About

sona mi

Hi! I'm DigiDuncan, a digital content creator.

I took on the name DigiDuncan in 2011, and since then have been dedicated to making anything creative; videos, livestreams, games, apps, you name it. My goals and ideals have definitely shifted a lot throughout that time (I was 12 when I started taking this seriously), I've never stopped wanting to make the best possible things for people with the skills I have.

My Discord bio for years has been:
Semi-professional game dev, video editor, and programmer. Minecraft nerd, rhythm gamer, VRChat content creator, doer of things and starter of projects. List incomplete.
and I do think that sums me up pretty well, but there's more than I can fit in a bio, which is I suppose what a website is for!

I do video editing, subtitling, graphic design, game dev, streaming, Discord bots, writing, and more! I really do find it hard to remember everything I'm up to.
